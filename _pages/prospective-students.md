<p class="ps-status"><strong>Status:</strong> Accepting applications from BSc thesis groups at Northern University Bangladesh.</p>

This page is for Northern University Bangladesh students interested in doing their BSc thesis under my supervision. Because I receive many requests, I use the form below to understand each group before we meet.

### Research areas I currently supervise
- Federated and privacy-preserving learning (medical imaging, remote sensing)
- Medical image analysis and computer vision
- Object detection, including tiny objects in UAV / satellite imagery
- EEG and biomedical signal analysis
- Explainable and trustworthy AI (XAI, fairness)

See my [publications](/publications/) for examples of past student work.

### What I expect from a thesis group
- Genuine interest in research, not only in completing the course requirement
- Willingness to read papers regularly and learn new tools (Python, PyTorch)
- Consistent weekly progress and honest communication
- Commitment to writing a paper if the results are good

### What you can expect from me
- Help choosing a novel, achievable research problem
- Regular feedback on experiments, writing and presentations
- Guidance through the full publication process

### How to apply
1. Form your group (usually around 3 members). **Only one member, the group leader, submits the form.**
2. Complete the **research task** in the form: read one recent paper and identify its limitations. Expect it to take about a week. Please write in your own words.
3. I review every submission and contact shortlisted groups by email for a short meeting, where all members should be able to explain the task.

Incomplete forms, or forms without the research task, will not be considered.
