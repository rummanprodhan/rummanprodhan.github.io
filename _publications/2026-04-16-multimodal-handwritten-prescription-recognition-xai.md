## Abstract
Current deep learning models often struggle with prescription-specific challenges, particularly in distinguishing visually similar drug names and domain abbreviations due to illegible handwriting and limited visual context. Resolving these ambiguities is critical for minimizing medication errors and ensuring patient safety within automated healthcare workflows. To address this, this study proposes a multimodal framework that fuses visual features from pretrained Convolutional Neural Networks, specifically ResNet50, EfficientNet-B3 and VGG16, with structured metadata embeddings for drug category, dosage form and over-the-counter status. Evaluated on the Doctors Handwritten Prescription BD dataset comprising 4,680 word images across 78 drug names, the approach utilizes pharmaceutical context to resolve visual conflicts while incorporating SHAP analysis to provide interpretable explanations for model decisions. The Multimodal ResNet50 model achieved superior performance with 94.49% accuracy, 94.99% precision, 94.49% recall and a 94.39% F1 score, demonstrating that integrating metadata significantly outperforms image-only baselines and enhances transparency for clinical adoption.

**Index Terms:** Handwritten Prescription Recognition, Drug Name Recognition, Multimodal Deep Learning, ResNet-50, EfficientNet, VGG16, Explainable AI (XAI), OCR, AI in Healthcare.
